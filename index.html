Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Via Tours & Travels customize our travel itinerary?

Every journey begins with understanding your preferences. After you submit your inquiry or speak with our travel concierge, we assign a dedicated senior destination architect who crafts a bespoke day-by-day itinerary including flights, 5-star resort options, private transfers, and handpicked local experiences.

Do you provide Visa documentation and appointment assistance?

Yes! We provide complete end-to-end visa assistance for Schengen (Europe), UK, USA, UAE, Vietnam, Singapore, Japan, and 80+ other destinations. We guide you through document preparation, cover letters, appointment bookings, and flight/hotel proof vouchers.

What is your Flexible Date Change policy?

We provide 100% Free Date Changes up to 21 days prior to your departure date across all our customized private tour packages with zero administrative penalties.

Can we personalize special dietary preferences (Vegetarian, Jain, Halal, Vegan)?

Absolutely. Prior to your departure, our concierge coordinates directly with executive chefs at your 5-star resorts, private cruises, and booked fine-dining restaurants to guarantee seamless vegetarian, Jain, Halal, vegan, or allergen-free meals throughout your stay.

How does 24/7 dedicated concierge support work while we are traveling?

You will have direct 24/7 WhatsApp and phone access to your dedicated senior travel specialist throughout your journey, paired with local on-ground representatives ready to assist with restaurant reservations, schedule tweaks, or emergency assistance in real time.

2026 — 1,400+ VIP Escapes & AI Curation

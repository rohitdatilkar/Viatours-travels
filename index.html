About Via Tours & Travels

Welcome to Via Tours & Travels, your premier destination for bespoke luxury travel, private villa retreats, and white-glove concierge itineraries.

Since our founding, we have curated extraordinary journeys across the world's most captivating corners — from private coral atolls in the Maldives and snow-draped alpine chalets in Zermatt, to sacred water temples in Ubud and ultra-luxury desert sanctuaries in Dubai.

Welcome to Via Tours & Travels. By using our website and submitting enquiries, you agree to the following terms and conditions:

1. Services Provided

Via Tours & Travels provides luxury travel consultation, bespoke itinerary planning, and booking coordination for domestic and international tours. All financial transactions are handled securely between the client and our agency via verified banking channels or payment links.

2. Bookings & Enquiries

When you submit a "Plan My Trip" enquiry, our team provides an itemized quotation. A booking is confirmed after an advance deposit is acknowledged by Via Tours & Travels.

3. Pricing & Currency

Package prices are listed in Indian Rupees (INR ₹) and are subject to availability and seasonal tariff adjustments. Applicable statutory taxes (such as 5% GST) are itemized in your quotation.

Travelers must hold passports with at least 6 months validity from return date. We provide comprehensive visa paperwork assistance for all our destinations.

5. Cancellations & Refunds

Cancellation policies depend on the respective airline, private charter, and 5-star hotel partners and will be provided in writing before confirmation.

1. Scope & Contractual Acceptance

These Terms & Conditions of Service (“Agreement”) govern the relationship between Via Tours & Travels (“the Agency”, “we”, “us”) and the lead traveler or designated entity booking our services (“Client”, “you”). Since 2009, our agency has designed high-touch, tailor-made journeys, private aviation and yacht charters, luxury accommodations, and curated destination experiences across the world.

By requesting a custom itinerary, placing a booking deposit, approving a written quotation, or accessing our platform (viatoursandtravels.com), you explicitly accept and agree to be bound by these terms on behalf of yourself and all accompanying travelers in your party.

2. Bespoke Itinerary Proposals & Quotations

Our travel architects craft detailed, day-by-day itineraries tailored to your unique travel desires, party size, and hospitality tier. Quotations provided by Via Tours & Travels are subject to the following principles:

  • Validity Window: Unless otherwise specified in writing, custom quotations remain valid for seven (7) calendar days from the date of issue. Commercial airline seats, private villa holds, and charter slots cannot be held indefinitely without deposit.
  • Dynamic Availability: Luxury boutique suites, overwater bungalows, and scheduled regional flights are subject to live inventory. If a preferred property becomes unavailable prior to deposit confirmation, we will substitute an accommodation of equal or superior standard.
  • Written Confirmation: An itinerary proposal becomes a binding booking only upon our formal issuance of a Booking Confirmation Note following deposit receipt.

3. Booking Deposits & Payment Milestones

To lock in luxury hotels, private chauffeurs, domestic flights, and exclusive guides, payments are structured according to transparent milestones:

Booking Stage Payment Timeline Percentage Required Operational Purpose
Initial Commitment Deposit Upon formal approval of itinerary 25% – 30% of total tour cost Secures non-refundable flight seats, private villa deposits, and peak-season hotel allocations.
Interim Stage Milestone 45 days prior to departure date 40% of total tour cost Finalizes luxury rail (e.g. Glacier Express), private yacht charters, internal flights, and specialized permits.
Final Settlement 21 days prior to departure date Remaining balance (30% – 35%) Releases complete travel dossier, digital vouchers, driver contact details, and emergency concierge codes.
Peak Holiday / Festive Season Bookings spanning Dec 15 – Jan 10 or major events 50% deposit; balance at 45 days Mandated by 5-star island resorts and European alpine chalets with non-negotiable holiday prepayment rules.

Payment may be remitted via approved corporate bank transfer (NEFT / RTGS / IMPS), wire transfer (SWIFT), or secure PCI-DSS compliant credit/debit card channels. All payments must be received in the invoiced currency (INR, USD, EUR, or AED).

4. Inclusions, Exclusions, Taxes & Currency

We pride ourselves on complete pricing transparency with no hidden surprises:

  • Standard Inclusions: Accommodations in specified categories, daily breakfast (and additional meal plans as detailed), private air-conditioned vehicle transfers with professional chauffeurs, sightseeing excursions with certified English-speaking local guides, and monument entrance fees outlined in your voucher.
  • Standard Exclusions: International long-haul airfares (unless explicitly itemized), personal visa and consular application fees, mandatory international travel insurance, discretionary hotel incidentals (mini-bar, spa, laundry), and customary gratuities for drivers and local escorts.
  • Statutory Indian Taxes (GST & TCS): Invoices for Indian residents are subject to applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST). International outbound packages booked under the Reserve Bank of India’s Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) are subject to statutory Tax Collected at Source (TCS) at prevailing rates (5% or 20%), fully creditable against your personal annual income tax filing.
  • Currency Exchange Stability: Once your initial deposit is credited, your quoted rate is locked against currency exchange fluctuations, protecting you from sudden currency market volatility.

5. Itinerary Alterations & Client Changes

We recognize that elite travelers appreciate flexibility. Should you desire adjustments to your itinerary after confirmation:

  • Bespoke Tweaks: Minor modifications (e.g., rescheduling a museum tour, swapping a restaurant reservation, or upgrading a room category) are accommodated with zero administrative fees, subject solely to actual supplier rate differences.
  • Significant Rescheduling: Changing primary travel dates or substituting entire cities within 30 days of departure may incur supplier cancellation penalties and rebooking fees levied by airlines and luxury properties.
  • Amendments by the Agency: While rare, should an unforeseen operational issue affect a confirmed partner (e.g., hotel maintenance or flight schedule cancellations), our senior concierge will arrange an equivalent or superior alternative at no additional expense to you.

6. Cancellation Schedule & Refund Rules

Should circumstances require you to cancel your bespoke journey, written notification must be submitted by email to our reservations desk (bookings@viatoursandtravels.com). The cancellation scale is determined by the date written notice is received:

Written Cancellation Notice Agency Retention Fee Eligible Client Refund
60 or more days prior to departure 10% of total package cost (plus non-refundable supplier outlays) 90% of refundable balance
30 to 59 days prior to departure 25% of total package cost (plus non-refundable flight tickets) 75% of refundable balance
15 to 29 days prior to departure 50% of total package cost 50% of refundable balance
14 days or fewer prior to departure / No-Show 100% of total package cost 0% (Suppliers fully committed)

Supplier Pass-Through Notice: Certain specialized components—including chartered flights, private safari concessions in Africa, high-season Maldives villas, and non-refundable commercial airfare classes—are governed by strict non-refundable terms dictated by the provider. Such components are communicated transparently in your initial quote.

7. Passports, Visas & Health Clearance

International travel necessitates strict adherence to destination sovereign immigration regulations:

  • Passport Validity: All travelers must possess an official passport valid for at least six (6) months beyond the scheduled return date, containing at least two blank consecutive visa endorsement pages.
  • Visa Facilitation: While Via Tours & Travels offers concierge visa advisory and documentation assistance, the granting or rejection of visas remains the sole sovereign discretion of the respective embassy or consulate. We cannot accept liability for visa processing delays or refusals.
  • Health & Transit Mandates: Clients are responsible for fulfilling all entry requirements, including yellow fever vaccinations, transit visas, electronic travel authorizations (eTA/ESTA), and return ticket proof.

8. Comprehensive Travel Insurance

For your financial peace of mind, Via Tours & Travels strongly advises all travelers to secure comprehensive international travel and medical insurance prior to embarkation. Ideal coverage should encompass:

  • Trip cancellation, curtailment, and flight delay reimbursements.
  • Emergency medical treatment, hospitalization, and international aeromedical evacuation.
  • Baggage loss, theft of personal luxury effects, and travel document replacement.
  • Coverage for specialized leisure activities (scuba diving, skiing, high-altitude trekking).

9. Supplier Relations, Liability & Force Majeure

Via Tours & Travels acts in the capacity of an elite travel architect and booking agent, contracting with reputable international airlines, luxury hotel corporations, licensed transport providers, and destination management companies:

  • Independent Contractors: All third-party providers operate under their own terms and licensing. The Agency exercises utmost care in supplier vetting but is not directly liable for acts of negligence, equipment failure, or service interruptions caused by independent suppliers.
  • Force Majeure: The Agency shall not be deemed in breach of this Agreement or held liable for non-performance resulting from circumstances beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to natural catastrophes, volcanic ash clouds, geopolitical conflicts, civil unrest, airline strikes, border closures, or government travel advisories.
  • Concierge Duty of Care: In the event of any Force Majeure event during your travel, our 24/7 senior crisis concierge will immediately intervene, advocating on your behalf with airlines and resorts to rearrange itineraries and safeguard your party.

10. Traveler Responsibilities & Local Laws

Travelers agree to abide by the local customs, environmental laws, and legal statutes of all visited destinations. Clients must refrain from conduct that causes disturbance, danger, or property damage to fellow travelers, local hosts, or wildlife reserves. We reserve the right to terminate the travel arrangements of any individual whose behavior is deemed unsafe, offensive, or hazardous, with zero refund obligation.

11. Dispute Resolution & Concierge Contacts

Should any dissatisfaction arise during your travel, you are urged to notify your assigned 24/7 Journey Concierge immediately so we can rectify the matter in real time. For formal post-journey reviews:

  • Amicable Resolution: All disputes shall first be submitted in writing to our Senior Guest Relations Desk (concierge@viatoursandtravels.com) for amicable settlement within fourteen (14) business days.
  • Governing Law & Jurisdiction: This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India. The courts of Bengaluru, Karnataka, India shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any legal proceedings arising hereunder.

1. Scope & Commitment to Discretion

Via Tours & Travels (“we”, “our”, or “the Agency”) operates as a premier bespoke travel concierge, organizing international holiday itineraries, five-star accommodations, private aviation and yacht charters, and premium destination experiences. Established in 2009, our agency has served high-net-worth individuals, discerning families, and private travelers across India and internationally with an uncompromised standard of privacy.

This Privacy & Data Protection Charter applies to all personal information gathered through our web platform (viatoursandtravels.com), direct telephone consultations, encrypted WhatsApp concierge channels, customized trip planning forms, and in-person bookings at our concierge suites. By engaging our services or providing your travel specifications, you acknowledge the privacy practices outlined herein.

3. How We Utilize Your Information

We process your personal information strictly based on legitimate contractual necessity and explicit client consent. Specific operational uses include:

  • Bespoke Itinerary Architecture: Curating tailor-made holiday proposals, multi-leg flights, and private ground transportation aligned with your wishes.
  • Reservation Procurement: Securing guaranteed rooms, villas, private yachts, safari game drives, and bilingual local guides with verified partners.
  • Real-Time Travel Concierge: Furnishing 24/7 client support during ongoing journeys, including emergency schedule changes, weather adjustments, and medical coordination.
  • Statutory & Immigration Compliance: Submitting mandatory passenger manifests to civil aviation bodies, maritime authorities, and national border control systems.
  • Client Care & Feedback: Conducting post-journey satisfaction reviews to continuously elevate our service standards and hospitality curation.

4. Sharing with Verified Travel Partners & Suppliers

Because luxury travel involves international logistics across multiple sovereign jurisdictions, selective data transmission is necessary to execute your travel reservations. We share only the minimum required data with:

  • Commercial Airlines & Private Charter Operators: Full names, passport details, and date of birth required for IATA e-ticketing and civil aviation security.
  • Five-Star Hoteliers & Luxury Resorts: Guest names, arrival timelines, and special dietary/honeymoon/anniversary preferences for VIP room allocation.
  • Licensed Destination Management Companies (DMCs): Contact numbers and flight arrival details shared with vetted local chauffeurs and guides for safe airport transfers.
  • Embassies, Consulates & Visa Outsourcing Agencies: Application documentation submitted strictly upon your explicit instruction for visa processing.

Our Zero-Monetization Pledge: Via Tours & Travels does not sell, trade, license, or exchange client names, phone numbers, or itinerary histories with third-party marketers, advertisers, credit brokers, or lead aggregators. Your information is accessed exclusively for executing your travel experience.

5. Cookie Policy & Client Preferences

Our website utilizes lightweight, privacy-focused cookies and local storage tokens designed to optimize your browsing journey. These include:

  • Essential Functional Cookies: Remembering your active currency selection (INR, USD, EUR, AED), dark/light theme preference, and custom trip planner progress.
  • Security & Performance Tokens: Preventing cross-site request forgery (CSRF), maintaining secure session state, and load balancing across content delivery networks.
  • Aggregated Analytics: Measuring anonymous page interaction metrics to identify popular destinations and optimize site speed without profiling individuals.

You can adjust your cookie settings through your browser preferences at any time. Disabling cookies will not restrict your ability to view destinations or submit customized trip inquiries.

6. Data Security & Storage Architecture

We deploy military-grade digital and physical safeguards to maintain confidentiality across our systems:

  • Cryptographic In-Transit Security: All data exchanged between your browser and our infrastructure is secured using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) with 256-bit encryption.
  • Storage Hardening: Cloud databases feature AES-256 encryption at rest, isolated VPC environments, and continuous automated backup snapshots.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Our database architecture implements strict Row-Level Security policies ensuring inquiries and client dossiers are isolated and protected against unauthorized access.
  • Access Restrictions: Only certified senior travel planners bound by strict non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) are granted role-based access to active client files.

7. Retention Periods & Safe Disposal

We retain personal information only for the duration necessary to satisfy the purposes described in this Charter:

  • Unconfirmed Trip Inquiries: Contact details and itinerary inquiries that do not result in a confirmed reservation are automatically purged after 18 months.
  • Confirmed Booking Records & Invoices: Financial transaction records, invoices, and payment receipts are retained for 7 financial years to comply with statutory taxation and audit mandates under Indian law.
  • Passport & Sensitive Visa Files: High-resolution passport copies gathered for visa filings are securely deleted from our working drives within 30 days after the conclusion of your journey, unless you request retention on file for expedited future travel.

8. Your Statutory Rights & Controls

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and international privacy standards, you hold comprehensive rights over your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Request a comprehensive summary or portable digital copy of all personal information we maintain regarding your profile and journeys.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: Amend outdated contact details, update passport renewals, or correct misspelled names prior to ticketing.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): Instruct us to erase your profile and contact records from our marketing and concierge databases, subject to statutory tax and aviation retention rules.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent for communications at any moment without penalty or cancellation fees for pre-existing travel bookings.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate an authorized representative to manage your travel records in the event of unforeseen incapacity.

9. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Via Tours & Travels arranges luxury journeys across Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Africa, the Americas, and the Indian Ocean. Delivering international reservations inherently necessitates transmitting passport details and passenger manifests to overseas suppliers located in destination nations (e.g., Switzerland, Maldives, UAE, France, Bali, UK, Japan).

We ensure all international hospitality networks, ground transfer partners, and airline partners are bonded and operate under binding contractual privacy commitments that mirror our strict security standards.

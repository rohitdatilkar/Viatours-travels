Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Via Tours & Travels customize our travel itinerary?

Every journey begins with understanding your preferences. After you submit your inquiry or speak with our travel concierge, we assign a dedicated senior destination architect who crafts a bespoke day-by-day itinerary including flights, 5-star resort options, private transfers, and handpicked local experiences.

Do you provide Visa documentation and appointment assistance?

Yes! We provide complete end-to-end visa assistance for Schengen (Europe), UK, USA, UAE, Vietnam, Singapore, Japan, and 80+ other destinations. We guide you through document preparation, cover letters, appointment bookings, and flight/hotel proof vouchers.

What is your Date Modification & Change policy?

Via Tours & Travels charges zero agency service or amendment fees for date adjustments requested up to 21 days prior to your departure date. Any fare differences, airline change charges, or resort seasonal tariff variances imposed directly by airlines or hotels apply as per their respective policies.

What is the deposit and payment schedule?

To confirm your luxury itinerary and secure resort allocations, we request a 30% advance deposit upon itinerary confirmation. The remaining 70% balance is payable 30 days prior to departure (or 45 days for peak festive seasons). Payments are accepted via verified institutional bank transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS) or secure payment link. All bookings include itemized GST invoices and statutory TCS documentation under Section 206C(1G) of the Income Tax Act.

How are GST and TCS calculated on international travel packages?

International tour packages attract statutory 5% GST without input tax credit. In addition, Tax Collected at Source (TCS) under Section 206C(1G) of the Income Tax Act applies at 5% on international tour packages up to ₹7,00,000 per individual per financial year under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), and 20% on amounts exceeding ₹7,00,000. TCS is not an additional cost; it is an advance tax credited directly to your PAN (reflecting in Form 26AS/AIS) which you can adjust against your annual tax liability or claim as a refund in your ITR. Please consult your chartered accountant or tax advisor regarding PAN linkage and LRS threshold limits.

Is international travel insurance required for our trip?

Comprehensive international travel insurance covering emergency medical expenses, overseas hospitalizations, trip cancellations, flight delays, and baggage loss is strongly recommended for all international departures. Our concierge can facilitate comprehensive policy options through licensed insurers.

Can we personalize special dietary preferences (Vegetarian, Jain, Halal, Vegan)?

Absolutely. Prior to your departure, our concierge coordinates directly with executive chefs at your 5-star resorts, private cruises, and booked fine-dining restaurants to guarantee seamless vegetarian, Jain, Halal, vegan, or allergen-free meals throughout your stay.

How does 24/7 dedicated concierge support work while we are traveling?

You will have direct 24/7 WhatsApp and phone access to your dedicated senior travel specialist throughout your journey, paired with local on-ground representatives ready to assist with restaurant reservations, schedule tweaks, or emergency assistance in real time.

Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: October 2026 • Via Voyages & Travel Services

Welcome to Via Tours & Travels (brand name operated by Via Voyages & Travel Services, registered office: Level 6, Prestige Meridian, 29 MG Road, Bengaluru 560001, Karnataka, India). By engaging our travel consultation services, requesting itinerary proposals, or confirming holiday bookings, you acknowledge and agree to the following terms and conditions:

1. Scope of Services & Legal Entity

Via Tours & Travels acts as an expert travel consultancy and booking coordinator. We curate bespoke luxury travel itineraries, book airline reservations via accredited ticketing channels, secure accommodation with vetted 5-star resort and villa partners, and orchestrate private chauffeured transfers, yacht charters, and local excursions.

2. Booking Procedure & Payment Terms

All travel inquiries submitted through our website or concierge desk receive a written, itemized itinerary proposal. To confirm your booking and initiate firm supplier allocations, the following payment schedule applies:

  • Advance Booking Deposit: 30% of the total package value upon approval of the customized itinerary.
  • Balance Payment: The remaining 70% balance is payable at least 30 days prior to your scheduled departure date (or 45 days prior for peak festive season travel between December 15 and January 15).
  • Late Bookings: Bookings requested within 30 days of departure require 100% payment upon confirmation.

Payments must be remitted via verified institutional banking mechanisms (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS) or authorized corporate payment gateway links. In strict compliance with Section 269ST of the Income Tax Act, 1961, cash transactions exceeding statutory limits will not be accepted.

3. Statutory Taxes: GST & TCS Disclosures

All package quotations and tax invoices clearly itemize applicable statutory government levies:

  • Goods and Services Tax (GST): In accordance with statutory Indian GST rules, international tour packages are subject to 5% GST without input tax credit.
  • Tax Collected at Source (TCS) under Section 206C(1G): TCS applies on all overseas tour packages under the Reserve Bank of India’s Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS). As per current provisions:
    • 5% TCS applies on cumulative overseas tour packages up to ₹7,00,000 per individual per financial year.
    • 20% TCS applies on amounts exceeding ₹7,00,000 per individual per financial year.

Important Note on TCS: TCS is not an additional operational charge or non-refundable fee. It is an advance direct tax credited against the primary traveler’s Permanent Account Number (PAN), reflecting in Form 26AS / Annual Information Statement (AIS). You can offset this credit against your advance tax/self-assessment tax liability or claim it as a refund when filing your annual Income Tax Return (ITR). Travelers are advised to consult their Chartered Accountant or tax advisor regarding PAN submission and annual LRS threshold tracking.

4. Date Modification & Amendment Policy

Via Tours & Travels provides flexible itinerary planning. We charge zero agency amendment or service fees for itinerary date adjustments requested at least 21 days prior to departure. Any fare differences, airline rescheduling fees, or hotel seasonal tariff variances imposed directly by airlines, private charters, or resorts will be communicated in writing and passed through transparently without markup.

5. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellations must be communicated to our concierge team in writing via email. Detailed cancellation schedules, refund timelines, and non-refundable component disclosures are governed by our dedicated Cancellation & Refund Policy, which forms an integral part of these Terms.

6. Passports, Visas & Consular Clearances

All international travelers must possess a passport with at least six (6) months validity beyond their intended return date and adequate blank pages. While Via Tours & Travels provides comprehensive visa documentation assistance, appointment scheduling, and voucher facilitation, the granting or rejection of any visa or transit permit rests exclusively within the sovereign authority of the respective embassy, high commission, or consulate.

7. Mandatory Travel Insurance Advisory

Comprehensive international travel insurance is strongly recommended for all international travelers. Adequate coverage should insure against emergency medical evacuation, hospitalization, overseas outpatient care, flight cancellations, trip interruptions, and baggage loss. Via Tours & Travels accepts no financial liability for uninsured medical or travel disruptions.

8. Force Majeure & Limitation of Liability

Via Tours & Travels shall not be held liable for failure to perform or delays in itinerary execution caused by events beyond reasonable control (Force Majeure), including but not limited to severe weather conditions, acts of God, civil unrest, flight cancellations or airspace closures by civil aviation authorities, pandemics, or government travel advisories. In such eventualities, we will exert all commercially reasonable efforts to secure supplier credits, alternative travel arrangements, or date reschedulings.

9. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

These terms and conditions and any disputes arising out of travel arrangements curated by Via Tours & Travels shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Courts in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear timelines, refund mechanisms, and date adjustment guidelines.

At Via Tours & Travels, we understand that travel plans may change due to unforeseen circumstances. We maintain complete transparency regarding our cancellation terms, supplier recovery, and refund processing mechanisms.

1. Cancellation Notice & Fee Schedule

Cancellation requests must be submitted in writing via email to concierge@viatoursandtravels.com from the primary email address registered on the booking. The effective date of cancellation is the date the written notice is received during Indian business hours.

2. Special Non-Refundable Components

Certain elements of bespoke luxury travel are subject to strict supplier cancellation rules independent of our standard schedule:

  • Airlines & Private Flights: Flight tickets (commercial scheduled flights or private air charters) are subject to the specific fare rules and cancellation penalties established by the issuing airline. Many promotional, premium economy, or business class fares carry strict non-refundable clauses.
  • Peak Holiday & Festive Season Bookings: Bookings falling between December 15 and January 15 (Christmas/New Year peak period) or during major regional events (e.g. Monaco Grand Prix, Swiss World Economic Forum) are 100% non-refundable once confirmed, due to mandatory non-refundable resort prepayment contracts.
  • Visa Processing Fees: Government consular visa fees and visa application center charges (e.g. VFS Global) are entirely non-refundable once documents have been submitted to the embassy.

3. Refund Processing Timelines & Methods

Once a written cancellation request is verified and processed:

  • Approved refunds will be processed within 10 to 14 business days.
  • Refunds are remitted strictly back to the original source bank account or payment method utilized during booking in compliance with statutory anti-money laundering regulations.
  • Statutory TCS amounts collected and remitted to the government cannot be refunded by the tour operator once the return has been filed; however, the traveler retains full advance tax credit under their PAN in Form 26AS/AIS for claiming against their annual tax liability or ITR refund.

4. Zero Agency Fee Date Changes

To provide flexibility, Via Tours & Travels charges zero agency service fees for itinerary date adjustments requested at least 21 days prior to departure. Any fare differences, airline change fees, or seasonal hotel rate adjustments imposed directly by suppliers will be charged at actuals with zero markup.

Travel Insurance Advisory: We strongly advise every traveler to obtain comprehensive travel cancellation insurance at the time of booking to cover non-refundable deposits in the event of personal emergencies, medical hospitalizations, or family exigencies.
